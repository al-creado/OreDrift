Ore Drift Privacy Policy

Ore Drift is a single-player idle game for Android. This policy explains what happens with information when you play. In short: the game itself does not ask for an account and does not collect personal data. The ads in the game are shown by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play. Both are described below.

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress (ore, upgrades, vessels, commendations, daily mission and supply streak progress, your sound and reminder settings, which shop items you own, order numbers of purchases already delivered, and the time you last played) is saved in the app's private storage on your device. The app does not send this information to the developer, and the developer cannot see it. It is used only to continue your game and to work out how much your fleet mined while the app was closed.

If automatic backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include this saved progress in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your own Google account settings. You can turn it off or delete it in your device's Google backup settings.

Ads

Ore Drift is free to play and shows ads served by Google AdMob:

  • a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen, and
  • optional rewarded video ads that are shown only when you choose to tap a button to watch one (for example to double your offline earnings).

To show, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, and how you interact with ads. This is done by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The developer does not receive your personal information from this.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. If it applies to you, you can change your choice later in Settings, under "Ad privacy choices".
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings, then Privacy or Google, then Ads).
  • The one-time purchase "Remove ads" removes the banner and stops the app from requesting ads.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as removing ads, ore packs, a starter bundle and a permanent boost) are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your payment details. The app asks Google Play which items your Google account owns so that it can restore them on a new phone and deliver purchases that were interrupted.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • The game itself does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for short haptic feedback when you tap. You can turn off touch vibration in your Android settings.
  • Internet access and advertising ID: used by Google AdMob and Google Play, as described above.
  • Notifications: optional. Only if you turn on Reminders in Settings does the app ask for permission. Reminders are scheduled on your device and say when your daily supply drop is ready, your hold is full or your cargo pod is ready. Turn them off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, then Wipe save, inside the app, or uninstall the app. Either removes the saved progress from your device. The developer holds no copy. If you used Android backup, delete that backup in your Google account settings as well. Data held by Google (ads, Google Play purchases) is managed in your Google account.

Children

Ore Drift is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app adds features that handle data differently (for example cloud saves), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
