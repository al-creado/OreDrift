Ore Drift Privacy Policy

Ore Drift is a single-player idle game for Android. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: Ore Drift does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress (ore, upgrades, vessels, commendations, daily mission and supply streak progress, your sound and reminder settings, and the time you last played) is saved in the app's private storage on your device. The app does not send this information anywhere, and the developer cannot see it. It is used only to continue your game and to work out how much your fleet mined while the app was closed.

If automatic backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include this saved progress in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your own Google account settings. You can turn it off or delete it in your device's Google backup settings.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files or device identifiers.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • The game itself makes no network requests, so no information is transmitted by the app to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for short haptic feedback when you tap. You can turn off touch vibration in your Android settings.
  • Notifications: optional. Only if you turn on Reminders in Settings does the app ask for permission. Reminders are scheduled on your device and say when your daily supply drop is ready or your hold is full. Turn them off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, then Wipe save, inside the app, or uninstall the app. Either removes the saved progress from your device. The developer holds no copy. If you used Android backup, delete that backup in your Google account settings as well.

Children

Ore Drift does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever adds features that handle data (for example ads or cloud saves), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
